KF2-VR - READ ME FIRST
======================
KF2-VR plays your own copy of Killing Floor 2 in a PC VR headset.
Made by Kvasir94 and optimumbox. It is an early alpha fan project, not
affiliated with Tripwire Interactive. Expect bugs, and please report them.

YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11.
- Killing Floor 2 on Steam. (Epic works for Solo VR only, and is experimental.)
- Your headset's PC app running: SteamVR, Quest Link / Air Link, Virtual
  Desktop or similar. Desktop play works without a headset.

START
1. Right-click the ZIP > Extract All, into a new folder (Documents is fine).
   Don't run it from inside the ZIP.
2. Open Killing Floor 2 from Steam once, then quit it.
3. Double-click "Start KF2-VR". Leave the "app" folder alone.
4. The first time, it finds your game and asks a few setup questions (hand,
   seated or standing, movement, reloads, graphics). The recommended choices
   are fine to start with.
5. Press Quick start for a solo match, or pick Solo match, Host a game or
   Join a friend.

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS (Steam)
- Everyone needs this same KF2-VR version.
- Host: the first time, KF2-VR downloads the free KF2 game server (about 9 GB,
  32 GB once installed). Copy the join code and send it privately; it contains
  your password. Friends outside your home network need UDP 7777 and 27015
  forwarded to your PC.
- Join: copy your friend's whole code, press Paste, then Join.

GOOD TO KNOW
- In the headset, press Menu on your left controller (or hold both sticks)
  for settings, calibration and fit.
- Reload styles: Button (press X / A, the default), Manual (reload by hand)
  or Realistic. Change them in Settings.
- VAC is off, so KF2-VR only plays on VAC-off servers. Matches earn XP.
- Keep the KF2-VR window open while you play. When you quit the game, it puts
  Killing Floor 2 back to normal.

SOMETHING WENT WRONG?
- Help > Repair after a crash: quit KF2 first, then press it.
- Help > Save logs for a bug report makes a ZIP to share. Check it before
  sharing; nothing is uploaded automatically.
- Help > Report a bug opens the KF2-VR thread on the Flat2VR Discord. Say
  which headset and graphics card you use, the version shown in the window,
  Solo/Host/Join, the map, the weapon and what you were doing.
- If your store updates KF2, wait for a matching KF2-VR release. Never copy
  files between KF2-VR versions.

UPDATING
Extract each new version into its own new folder. Your settings carry over.

MORE
In the app\docs folder: CONTROLS-CARD.txt (quick controls), PLAYER-GUIDE.md
(full guide), VR_CONTROLS.md (every control) and RELEASE-NOTES-DRAFT.md
(what's new and known issues). The ZIP includes KF2-derived files, among them the
hands package; their rights stay with their owners. Licences are in
app\notices.
