KF2-VR - READ ME FIRST
======================

To play: double-click "Start KF2-VR".
That's the only file you need. Leave the "app" folder alone.

BEFORE THE FIRST TIME
---------------------
1. Unzip the whole ZIP somewhere easy, like your Desktop or Documents.
   (Right-click the ZIP > Extract All.) Don't run it from inside the ZIP.
2. Open normal Killing Floor 2 from your Steam or Epic launcher once, then quit it.
   Use Windows 10/11 and your own KF2 copy. Python is included in the ZIP;
   you do not need to install it separately.
3. Playing in VR? Turn on your headset and start its PC app
   (Quest Link / Air Link, SteamVR, or Virtual Desktop) before you start.
   Select that app as the active OpenXR runtime in its settings.
4. Everyone needs the same KF2-VR ZIP. When a new one comes out,
   unzip it as a new folder and use that.
5. KF2-VR is matched to the exact Steam and Epic builds in app\release.json. If your store
   updates KF2, the launcher will say your copy doesn't match until a new
   KF2-VR ZIP is out. Epic is experimental Solo VR only; see its steps below.

WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
This is an early alpha, tested so far on one developer PC with a Quest
headset over Link. Solo play is the most tested path. The developer has also
used a remotely joined hosted server; wider internet and hardware coverage
need feedback. Expect bugs, and please
report them. The default graphics are Performance at 75% render scale;
raise them in Settings or the headset menu if your PC has headroom.
The questions we most want answered are in app\docs\FEEDBACK-QUESTIONS.md.

PLAY SOLO FIRST
---------------
1. Start KF2-VR. Auto uses a sole installation or the last valid folder
   in this extracted release. With both stores installed, choose Steam for
   these steps (or Epic for its steps below). Pick "VR headset" or "Desktop - no headset", then "Play solo".
2. Pick a map and difficulty, click "Start", then choose your perk and Ready.
   Solo needs no server download or port forwarding. Start with the defaults.

EPIC GAMES - EXPERIMENTAL SOLO VR
-------------------------------
Epic is experimental and has not passed overall headset acceptance.
The reconciled stock-menu startup in this ZIP still needs a headset retest.
Earlier testing confirmed partial controller/gameplay functionality,
including Outpost, 1858/Deagle tracking and trader access. Overall usability
remains incomplete; this ZIP is not established as a runtime pass.

1. Connect your headset and OpenXR runtime. In Start KF2-VR select Epic Games,
   then Play solo and Start. Keep the KF2-VR window open throughout the session.
2. Click Copy Epic Launch Options. In Epic Library > Killing Floor 2 > Manage,
   save any previous Launch Options text locally, replace it with the session
   line, switch ON and click Launch in Epic within five minutes.
   Your official Epic launcher handles authentication; don't copy account tokens.
3. At the stock game menu choose Play Solo Offline, map, difficulty and length,
   then your perk and Ready. Launcher selections also populate the headset's
   LOCAL MATCH > START LOCAL MATCH > START SOLO MATCH route; no automatic map launch.
4. Quit KF2 normally. The launcher restores its temporary native files.
   Remove the session line from Epic Launch Options and restore your previous
   text/switch setting before ordinary play. After an interruption, close KF2
   and select Epic > Fix a stuck session. Unknown DLLs stay preserved for review.

Epic Host/Join/Desktop, cross-store online play, local test control, motion
recording and highlight logging are unavailable. Optional content is unaccepted.
Primary-hand lever cycling is missing: the gun hand cannot yet leave the firing
grip to operate the lever while the support hand holds the rifle. The proposed
off-hand workaround is not headset-confirmed. Keep Button reload mode if physical
cycling blocks play. The VR Use/hold prompt correction also needs retesting.

JOIN A FRIEND
-------------
Steam only in this alpha.
1. Your friend sends you a code that starts with KF2VR1:
2. Start KF2-VR, pick "VR headset" or "Desktop", click "Join a friend".
3. Click "Paste code", then "Join".
4. In the lobby, pick your perk and press Ready.

HOST A GAME
-----------
Steam only in this alpha.
1. Start KF2-VR, click "Host a game", pick a map and difficulty, click Start.
2. The first time, KF2-VR downloads the free KF2 game server (about 32 GB).
   That can take a long time. It only happens once.
3. When it says your game is up, click "Copy code" and send it to your friends
   in a private message. The code has your game's password in it.
4. Friends on other internet connections need your router to forward
   UDP ports 7777 and 27015 to your PC. Friends on your home network don't.
   Allow KFServer through Windows Firewall when prompted. Joiners do not need
   to forward ports or download the server.

Use Solo or the launcher's VAC-off hosted games. Do not join VAC-secured servers.
Custom multiplayer does not promise ordinary ranked XP or perk progression.

WHILE YOU PLAY
--------------
Keep the KF2-VR window open. When you quit the game, KF2-VR puts
Killing Floor 2 back to normal. The window says when it's done.

VR controls: aim with your hands, trigger fires, A/X reloads, grip grabs.
One stick walks, the other turns. Quick card: app\docs\CONTROLS-CARD.txt.
Full binds, equipment gestures, calibration and tips: app\docs\VR_CONTROLS.md.
Syringe: choose it on the wheel, or double-tap Y/B on an already empty hand.
There is no wrist syringe pouch. Wheel choices confirm on trigger release.
Chest grenade: empty hand, hold grip at the grenade, swing and release grip.
Physical reloads: VR SETTINGS > INTERACTION > RELOAD MODE (Button by default).

SOMETHING WENT WRONG?
---------------------
Click "Save logs for a bug report" in the KF2-VR window. It makes a ZIP of
your recent logs, copies the ZIP, and opens the folder it's in. Report copies
replace personal paths, known usernames/player names, account IDs and network
addresses; passwords, recognized tokens and join codes are removed before zipping.
Original logs stay on your PC. Configs and crash dumps are not included.
Review the ZIP before sharing: free-form messages can contain other personal
details. Keep sensitive reports private.
Post public bug reports at github.com/Kvasir94/Killing-Floor-2-VR/issues
with your headset, graphics card and what you were
doing when it went wrong. Keep credentials and raw logs/configs/dumps private.
Nothing is uploaded automatically.

If KF2 crashed or your normal KF2 acts strange, quit KF2 first, then click
"Fix a stuck session". Keep this extracted folder until recovery completes.
After cleanup, start ordinary KF2 from your store to play without VR.

If the launcher says the game build does not match, do not copy DLLs from an
older ZIP or bypass the check. Save the error and wait for a matching release.
If it says package files are missing or changed, extract the complete ZIP into
a new folder. Keep the old folder until any stuck session has been restored.
Do not combine files from different releases; friends must use the same ZIP.

If VR cannot start, first check that your headset is connected in its PC app
and that the app is selected as the OpenXR runtime. Save the error and logs
before changing anything. A launcher error is not a reason to change headset
refresh rate or reset an otherwise connected headset.

For a useful report, include the build ID shown by the launcher, Solo/Host/Join,
map, weapon, the last action before the problem, and whether it happens again.
Keep join codes and passwords private. Do not attach raw session folders,
configs or crash dumps publicly; they have not been anonymized.

Each player needs their own Killing Floor 2 copy. Multiplayer is Steam-only in this alpha.
This is an early alpha test build. KF2-VR is a fan project and is not
affiliated with Tripwire Interactive.
