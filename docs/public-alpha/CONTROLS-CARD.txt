KF2-VR CONTROLS - QUICK CARD (Touch labels)
Steam: Solo/Host/Join VR or Desktop. Epic: experimental Solo VR only.
Epic requires manual session Launch Options in the official Epic launcher.
=========================================
Full guide and tips: app\docs\VR_CONTROLS.md. Saved settings override defaults.
Touch: X/Y left, A/B right. Index: A/B on each hand; use the menu chord.
Vive/WMR/select-only profiles do not have complete face-button bindings supplied;
do not assume the same wheel/reload controls without validating your runtime.

WEAPONS AND HANDS
  Trigger                 Fire/use that hand's equipped item. Open empty hand: interact.
  X / A short tap         Reload on release. Physical reloads use the current mechanism.
  X / A hold              Change a supported persistent mode once, or hold it and
                          freshly pull trigger for secondary fire. Release trigger
                          first if it was already held.
  Y / B tap               Previous item; opens the hand if no distinct previous exists.
  Y / B double tap        Stow an occupied hand. On an already empty hand: draw syringe.
  Y / B hold 0.25 s        Open that hand's wheel, then release the button.
  Point/move controller   TOGGLE grip: squeeze AND RELEASE trigger to confirm.
                          HOLD grip: point, press GRIP to draw now; release to toss.
                          Open a grip already held on entry before confirming.
                          Stick tilt does not select. Y/B or centre confirm cancels.
                          After closing the turn-hand wheel,
                          centre its stick before turning/jumping.
  Grip at support contact Hold two-hand support by default; release grip to detach.
                          Acquired firearm support stays held across distance drift;
                          valid tracking is required.
                          TOGGLE grip: primary weapons stay held when fingers relax.
  Local toss/catch        HOLD grip: deliberate release tosses the same owned weapon.
                          Fresh free-hand squeeze catches nearby, either hand.
                          Misses return after the existing delay; ammo stays intact.
  Support-only carry      Release primary while holding support; operate bolt/pump
                          with freed hand. Squeeze its primary contact to regrip.
  Y / B at temple         Toggle flashlight instead of quick-swap.
  Wheel + submenu         Drop the held item (stock restrictions), God Mode, Back.

HEAL, GRENADE, MELEE
  Syringe                 Select it on the wheel or double-tap an already empty hand.
                          No wrist/forearm pouch. Hand near upper chest + trigger:
                          self-heal; otherwise aim and trigger for teammate healing.
                          X/A + fresh trigger also requests self-heal. Stow/swap after.
  Chest grenade           Empty the hand and release support; palm to visible grenade,
                          squeeze/hold GRIP, swing, RELEASE GRIP to throw/drop.
                          Trigger does not throw. No wheel fallback grenade throw.
  Empty fist              Clench grip and swing to punch. Hold trigger while clenched
                          to charge, then punch while holding. Open/release cancels.
  Empty-fist guard         Raise both clenched empty fists; lower/re-raise to rearm.
  Melee/gun bash           Swing for physical contacts. Two-hand guards brace across
                          body; button-guard profiles use X/A, with X/A+trigger for
                          supported secondary/heavy actions. Ion Thruster: X/A ultimate.
  Teammate gestures        Multiplayer empty fists bump, open hands high-five.
  Zed/body grab            Empty hand + held grip near eligible target; release to
                          drop/throw. On in Solo; online the host decides.
  Dosh                    Stock TossMoney (default KEYBOARD B); no active hip wallet.
                          VR controller B still controls right-hand inventory.

MOVE AND MENU (DEFAULT MOVEMENT HAND LEFT)
  Left stick              Head-relative walking. Click while deflected latches sprint;
                          centre or crouch ends it. Fire/reload/draw can pause sprint.
  Right stick             Left/right: 30-degree snap turn. Outer rim up: jump, or stand
                          from stick crouch first. Outer rim down: crouch toggle.
  Right-stick click       Close the movement-hand wheel if open. Otherwise near a
                          closed/broken door: draw/return welder in RIGHT hand;
                          elsewhere: open the movement-hand wheel.
                          Trigger welds; A+trigger unwelds. No stick bash.
  Movement-hand wheel     Smooth walking stays available while open; teleport is blocked.
  Physical crouch         Uses captured height; seated/button crouch is separately set.
  Teleport mode           Movement-stick deflection aims arc; RETURN TO CENTRE commits
                          a valid destination. Movement-stick CLICK cancels.
  Menu                    Menu button, or centred sticks + released triggers + both
                          stick clicks held 0.6 s. Solo menu pauses when possible;
                          online keeps running. Pointer trigger presses menu targets.
  Trader                  Enter trader pod and use its floating stock menu.

SETTINGS AND SPECIAL ITEMS
  Headset menu > Settings: Comfort, Hands & Weapons, Graphics & HUD and Audio,
  the same pages as the launcher. Calibration and fit live in the headset menu.
  Reload mode: Button (X / A, the default), Manual (reload by hand; an empty
  trigger starts the reload) or Realistic (also rack a loaded gun; eject the old
  mag yourself). Ammo rules show with Manual or Realistic.
  Hip reloads (Manual/Realistic): with the other hand busy, press the gun hand's
  reload button and bring the gun to its hip loader.
  Fresh graphics: Quality / 100%, DLSS Auto. Saved preferences win.
  Movement/dominant-hand settings do not swap physical face buttons; welder is right.
  Fun & Cheats opens practice tools; they make the session unranked.
  C4/Sentinel/Bombardier: hold trigger, swing, release to throw/place; X/A+trigger detonate.
  RAVEN-7: hold trigger, swing, release to throw. Fresh airborne hold suspends; release
  recalls. Resting axe: fresh press recalls. Fresh grip is a recall shortcut.
  Breacher is omitted and its controls are hidden in this stable candidate.

Desktop uses stock KF2 bindings; full guide lists default keyboard controls.
Keep the launcher open for cleanup. After a crash use Help > Repair after a crash.
Save logs for a bug report; review the ZIP before sharing. See full guide for
build-specific report privacy, launch/Host/Join, recovery and troubleshooting.
